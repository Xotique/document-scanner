Document scanner for Android

Every page stays on your phone.

Scan Vault scans documents to PDF, reads the text on every page so you can search it, and compresses, signs and protects your files — all on the device. No account, no upload, no cloud.

  • No account
  • No upload

Android · Coming soon to Google Play

Nothing leaves your phone

Scanning, text recognition, compressing and password protection all run on the device. Turn on aeroplane mode and they still work.

No account, no storage access

There is nothing to sign up for. Files come in through Android's own picker, so the app only ever sees what you hand it.

Made for everyday phones

Pages are processed one at a time, so a forty-page scan does not run a 3 GB phone out of memory.

Text recognition

Find a document by what is written in it

Every page is read the moment it arrives — scans, photos and imported PDFs alike — so you can search your whole library for a name, an amount or a reference number.

  • Reading happens on the phone and needs no connection
  • Open the recognised text to read it or copy it out
  • Finding a document by its name is always free and unlimited

Compress

See the real size before you commit

Each quality is measured on pages from across your document, so the estimate is what you actually get. Pages are never enlarged, a setting that would not really help is never offered, and if the result is not meaningfully smaller your original stays exactly as it was.

  • High, Medium and Small, each with an honest estimate
  • Imported PDFs are re-drawn as images, and the app tells you before it happens

Share and save

One PDF, or a picture of every page

Send a document to any app, or save it to a folder you choose. It arrives named after your document, not a string of numbers.

  • Free copies carry a small mark in the corner — watch an ad to send one without it
  • Export every document at once to keep a backup

Tools

The rest of your paperwork, on the same phone

Sign with a signature you draw once. Put both sides of an ID card on one page at life size. Merge, split, watermark, add a password, or turn a scan into a PDF whose text you can select.

  • Import PDFs, photos and Word files
  • Turn a document into images, or photos into a PDF
  • Reorder, rotate, crop and insert pages, with undo

Private safe · Pro

A private safe for the documents that matter

One code — and your fingerprint or face — for everything you move into it. Documents in the safe disappear from your library, your search results and the recycle bin until you unlock it.

You will not find a screenshot of it here: the safe blocks screenshots and screen recording, and hides itself in the recent-apps switcher.

Code scanner

See what a code says before it opens

Scan Vault reads QR codes and barcodes, then shows the full address and calls out lookalike links, hidden destinations and other tricks — without looking anything up online. Nothing opens until you choose, and your last 200 scans stay on the phone.

One thing we will be straight about

Because nothing is uploaded, your documents exist only on this phone. Uninstalling the app, resetting the phone or losing it deletes them, and there is no copy anywhere for us to restore. Use Export every document in Settings to keep a backup of anything you would be sorry to lose.
